  1. Nature's Way of Canada

    Nature’s Way Canada, headquartered in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, is a trusted leader in natural health products with a purpose rooted in its belief: It’s in our nature to help people live healthier. For over 50 years, the company has combined traditional wisdom with modern science to deliver premium, science-backed natural health products – including omega-3s, vitamins, minerals, probiotics, and herbal remedies. Guided by its core values of being trustworthy, resourceful, and responsible, Nature’s Way Canada ensures integrity at every step – upholding rigorous quality standards and championing transparency that empowers consumers to make informed choices. Sustainability is central to its purpose: the company donates 1% of annual sales to environmental organizations, sources ingredients responsibly, uses post-consumer recycled packaging, and supports green energy projects across Canada. Recognized as one of Nova Scotia’s Top Employers, Nature’s Way Canada cultivates a workplace and community culture that champions wellness, innovation, and a deep respect for nature.

  15. Huangshan Qiyue Ecological Agriculture Co., Ltd.
